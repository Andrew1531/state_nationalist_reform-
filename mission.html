Strategic initiative overview

A structural intervention

Nationalist Reform is not just a movement; it is a structural intervention. We operate at the intersection of Constitutional Law and Private Equity, with the aim of separating individual wealth from state-managed liability.

The Trust Protocol is designed to help members operate in a private capacity while maintaining equitable control of their estates.

“Operating at the intersection of Constitutional Law and Private Equity.”

Principles

Stated goals

The four goals the initiative works toward. These describe intended aims, not guaranteed legal or tax outcomes.

1

Estate planning

Planning for how an estate is held and passed on, including its tax treatment.

2

Public nexus

Examining the claimed connection between public institutions and personal liability.

3

Private jurisdiction

Operating in a private capacity wherever applicable law allows.

4

Equitable title

Maintaining equitable control of estate assets through trust structures.

Results depend on applicable law and the facts of each situation. Consult a qualified attorney.
