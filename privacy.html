Privacy Policy

What information this site collects, why, and what we do with it. Effective November 25, 2026.

In short: If you send us your email address, we keep it only to communicate with you. We do not sell, rent or trade it, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

Information you give us

When you use the contact form, you give us your name, your email address, your question and, on some pages, a topic. That is the only personal information we ask you for.

How we use your email address

We use your name and email address only to communicate with you: to reply to your question and to continue a conversation you started. We do not use them for marketing, we do not sell, rent or share them with advertisers, and we do not sign you up for newsletters.

We keep your messages only as long as we need them to communicate with you. You can ask us to delete them at any time (see “Your choices” below).

How the contact form works

The form is processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), an outside service that delivers your message to our email inbox. Your message passes through FormSubmit and our email provider on its way to us. The form is not a confidential channel, so please don't include sensitive legal, financial or identity details.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it, such as which pages are viewed and roughly where visitors come from. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your IP address, browser and device type. We look at these reports only in aggregate, to improve the site. See how Google uses this data, or opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Other services that load on this site

Some pages load content from other companies, which receive your IP address and basic browser information when they do:

  • Google Fonts: the site's typefaces.
  • Rumble: the video on the home page.
  • jsDelivr: the chart on the Funding & Trust page.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Your choices

To find out what messages we hold from you, or to have them deleted, send a request through the contact form using the email address you used before. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and its effective date.
