The framework

Non-statutory status

The framework proposes holding assets in a foreign-domiciled trust entity. Whether that changes probate or tax treatment depends on applicable law and the facts — foreign trusts carry their own U.S. reporting and tax requirements.

Control

Equitable control

The stated aim is to keep equitable control of estate assets. Actual title and control depend on the trust documents and applicable law.
